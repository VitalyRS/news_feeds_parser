## [2026-07-01] II foro 'Cataluña, destino inversor', en imágenes
- **Article ID:** 109209
- **Source:** https://rss.elconfidencial.com/espana/
- **Original URL:** [https://www.elconfidencial.com/espana/cataluna/2026-07-01/cataluna-economia-resiliencia-inversion-bra_4381354/](https://www.elconfidencial.com/espana/cataluna/2026-07-01/cataluna-economia-resiliencia-inversion-bra_4381354/)

El Confidencial has organized the II edition of the forum 'Catalonia, investment destination' with the sponsorship of the Generalitat de Catalunya, the Barcelona Metropolitan Area (AMB), Banco Sabadell, CaixaBank and Moventia. During the event, public representatives, large companies and entrepreneurs spoke about Catalonia's leadership in various economic and productive areas, as well as the keys to its economic growth and which sectors are driving it. The event was closed by Alícia Romero, Councillor for Economy and Finance of the Generalitat de Catalunya, and featured the participation of Teresa García-Milà, President of the Cercle d'Economia; Javier Faus, President of Meridia Capital and Josep Ginesta, Secretary General of Pimec, among other speakers.
